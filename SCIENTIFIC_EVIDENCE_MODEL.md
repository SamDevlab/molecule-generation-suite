# Scientific evidence model

Research OS preserves the distinction between deterministic calculation,
machine learning, computational docking, physics simulation and experiment.
Evidence levels remain E0 heuristic, E1 ML, E2 computational, E3 physics,
E4 curated experimental and E5 validated experimental. Missing evidence is
never a pass, conditions and units are part of truth, and `INDETERMINATE`,
`OUT_OF_DOMAIN`, `FIRST_LOSS` and `FIRST_DIVERGENCE` are retained explicitly.
