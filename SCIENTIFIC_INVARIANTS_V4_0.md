# Scientific invariants — Research OS 4.0

The v4.0 invariant audit contains 31 checks and records zero violations.

It verifies, among other properties, that Codex cannot create Evidence or
change `EvidenceLevel`; E1/E2/E3/E4/E5 semantics remain canonical; docking
and Cantera retain their ceilings; OOD, uncertainty, missing data, units,
species and conditions remain explicit; source locators and dependency status
are preserved; claims, decisions, runs, bundles and the Ledger are append-only
or verifiable; program bounds and utility rejection remain active; and
conversation/source/prompt injection cannot override scientific state.

The audit is generated by
`tools/benchmark/master_validation_v40.py` and stored at
`.research-os-live-4.0/scientific-invariant-audit.json`.
