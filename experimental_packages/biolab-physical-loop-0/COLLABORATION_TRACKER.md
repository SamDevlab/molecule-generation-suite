# Collaboration Tracker

This is a versioned coordination template, not a CRM. No contact is recorded
until an attributable organization and contact date are supplied. Do not add
personal sensitive data.

Allowed `engagement_type` values are `COMMERCIAL_SERVICE` and
`SCIENTIFIC_COLLABORATION`.

| organization | contact_role | country | contact_date | status | engagement_type | requested_information | next_action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Allowed statuses: `NOT_CONTACTED`, `CONTACTED`, `AWAITING_REPLY`,
`NEEDS_INFORMATION`, `TECHNICAL_REVIEW`, `QUOTE_RECEIVED`,
`COLLABORATION_DISCUSSION`, `DECLINED`, `CLOSED`.
