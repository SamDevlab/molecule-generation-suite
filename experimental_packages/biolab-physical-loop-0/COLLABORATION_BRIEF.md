# Biolab Experimental Collaboration Brief

## What is being requested

Research OS has reached the boundary where the next decision-changing
evidence must come from a physical experiment. BIOEXP-001 is a vendor-neutral
request to measure aqueous solubility for a frozen four-compound 2×2 panel.

## Why this is not another computational campaign

The current local evidence ceiling is E2 computational. Repeating docking or
generating another molecular series would not close the open experimental gap.
The four cells are retained as a balanced factor design so that a laboratory
can measure factor A, factor B and their interaction under one declared
protocol.

## What a collaborator receives

The package contains exact isomeric identities, InChIKeys, formulas, SDF/MOL
structures, 2D structure images, a request document, a result template and a
manifest of artifact hashes. It is designed to be reviewed by a scientific
collaborator or laboratory without reading the repository.

## What the collaborator could provide

Please propose feasibility, commercial or collaboration pathway, sample
requirements, a complete protocol, analytical method, replicate policy,
conditions, raw-data/report format and attributable batch documentation.
Protocol review and freeze must occur before any real result is ingested.

## Interpretation boundary

Positive, negative, failed or non-quantifiable measurements can all be useful
if their conditions and provenance are recorded. This package does not claim
drug discovery or clinical efficacy. No real experiment has yet been
performed, no E4 evidence exists, and generation remains blocked until valid
external feedback is available.
