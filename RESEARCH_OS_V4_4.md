The v4.4 review examined 13 major programs from v3.9 through v4.3. Each review
contains a before snapshot, after snapshot, outcome references, scientific
changes, unchanged areas, cost without gain, blocked paths and a grounded next
action.

Results:

- 4 reviews identify knowledge change.
- 1 review identifies a decision change.
- 6 reviews refine or resolve a gap boundary.
- 2 reviews explicitly identify `NO_MATERIAL_CHANGE`.
- 5 reviews preserve blocked external paths.
- 2 reviews identify redundant work avoided.
- 2 reviews refine uncertainty/applicability boundaries.

No universal impact score is calculated. A program that executes correctly but
does not change the scientific state remains visible as `NO_MATERIAL_CHANGE`.
