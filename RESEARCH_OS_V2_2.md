# Research OS v2.2 — domain foundations

The application layer now has explicit boundaries for electrochemistry,
batteries, corrosion, mechanics, surfaces, transport, composites, ceramics,
additive manufacturing, aerospace, automotive and energy. They share one
condition-aware foundation contract and do not duplicate scientific logic.

Each foundation requires property, value, unit, conditions and method. Missing
conditions is insufficient evidence; missing method is indeterminate. A
foundation observation never pretends to be an engine-backed E3 result.

The priority order is electrochemistry, batteries, corrosion, mechanics and
transport. Application Labs remain composition/orchestration boundaries around
the existing Fuel, Combustion, Thermal, Propulsion, Metal and Degradation Labs.
