# Docking validation summary v1

The current Research OS docking campaign is complete as a bounded validation
exercise. REDOCK demonstrates pose localization within its cognate benchmark
envelope; CROSSDOCK provides partial evidence for non-cognate holo docking;
APODOCK does not demonstrate reliable rigid-apo pose recovery under the
evaluated protocols. All conclusions remain E2 computational.

The formal machine-readable boundary is the Docking Capability Profile v1.
No result is interpreted as affinity, efficacy, clinical evidence, or
experimental structural truth. Indeterminate execution and analysis outcomes
are retained rather than excluded.
