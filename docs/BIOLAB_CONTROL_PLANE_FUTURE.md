# Biolab / OmniHub control-plane future contract

This document is a future contract only. MOLDISC-019 does not implement an
OmniHub integration or a dashboard.

## Sam / Central

Sam / Central sets the human objective, budget, constraints, and approval
policy. It supervises external commitments and remains the authority for
material actions involving people, money, laboratories, or samples.

## OmniHub

OmniHub will eventually show current programs, jobs, blockers, cost,
experimental dependencies, evidence state, and agent activity. It is an
operational observation and coordination plane, not a scientific evidence or
claim authority.

## Biolab

Biolab owns the scientific next-action proposal: it reads current scientific
state, registered gaps, and capability boundaries, then proposes compute,
external evidence, experiment, or stop.

## Research OS

Research OS owns scientific validity: evidence levels, provenance, identity
gates, artifact hashes, lineage, reproducibility, dependency assessment,
stop conditions, and the rules preventing a synthetic fixture from becoming
E4 or duplicate evidence from becoming E5.

## Future dashboard fields

The eventual view should include current objective, scientific phase, active
campaign, evidence ceiling, next required evidence, blocked reason, compute
jobs, external experiments, budget consumed, results received, open claims,
open gaps, and the next-action recommendation. No UI is part of MOLDISC-019.
